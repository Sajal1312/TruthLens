Inclusion approval means the fixed claim/evidence bundle is suitable for annotation; it does not certify that the claim is true. Source independence is not established by publisher names or URL counts.

## How to complete the five reviews

1. Save your own copy of this document. Open each source link and compare its wording and surrounding qualifications with the saved passage.
2. For each candidate, choose one inclusion decision: `approved`, `revision_required`, or `rejected`. Enter your own identity/alias, actual timestamp with timezone and rationale. No choice is preselected.
3. Transfer your own assessments into the linked original `review.json` only when you are ready. Complete every evidence status, date and provenance field using the [review instructions](../README.md). This document itself is not submittable.
4. Do not invent publication days. Unknown dates can be retained with a rationale. Updates, event dates and month-only dates are distinct from original publication days. If a candidate needs changed evidence, request a versioned revision before approval.
5. This task has not submitted or imported anything. Keep interpretive review notes away from later blinded annotators.

Blank lines below are for the human reviewer alone. The batch manifest records hashes of saved candidate/evidence/check records and packets; it is not a checksum of live webpages.
