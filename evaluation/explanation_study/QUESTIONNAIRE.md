# Evidence explanation questionnaire

Read the claim, saved evidence and displayed assessment. Rate the presentation
you see, using only the supplied material. There are no required answers;
leave any question blank if you prefer. Do not include personal information.

For every task use: **1 strongly disagree; 2 disagree; 3 neither agree nor
disagree; 4 agree; 5 strongly agree.**

1. The explanation is clear (`clarity`).
2. The explanation is useful for examining this claim (`usefulness`).
3. The presentation helps me identify limitations of the evidence (`limitations_help`).
4. I understand why the displayed verdict was reached (`verdict_understanding`).
5. I would rely on this assessment without checking the underlying evidence (`reliance_without_checking`).

Then answer the two displayed multiple-choice questions: one about the reason
the system recorded and one about interpreting evidence-assessment limitations.
How confident are you, from **0 to 100**, that your answer to the second
question is correct? This asks about your quiz answer, not claim truth.

Optional: what was helpful or confusing? Do not enter personal details.

Timing fields, if used, are completed by a facilitator using a stopwatch.
Leave unmeasured times blank. Do not guess elapsed time from memory.

When finished, explicitly download your responses and choose whether to share
the file with the organizer. No answers are revealed during the tasks.
